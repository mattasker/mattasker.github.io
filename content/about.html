About

I'm a theoretical biologist with a background in physics and applied mathematics. I work with Arne Traulsen in the Department of Theoretical Biology at the Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Biology.

Before moving to Plön, I completed a PhD in applied mathematics at the University of Leeds, supervised by Mauro Mobilia and Alastair Rucklidge. My thesis studied the evolution of populations in fluctuating environments. I previously studied theoretical physics at the University of Manchester.
